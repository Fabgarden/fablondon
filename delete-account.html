How to submit a request

  1. Send an email to hello@menoova.it.
  2. Use the subject: Delete my SnapAccounts account.
  3. Send the request from the same email address used for your SnapAccounts account.
Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the account and account information held by our authentication service, except information we must retain for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons.

Data stored on your device

Expense records, folders and suppliers are currently stored locally on your device. To remove local app data, delete the records in the app where available and uninstall SnapAccounts from the device.

Processing time

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you if additional verification is needed.
