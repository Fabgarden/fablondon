This policy explains how SnapAccounts handles information when you create an account, scan a receipt or use the app.

1. Who we are

SnapAccounts is a Menoova product. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@menoova.it.

2. Information we process

Account information

When you register, we process your email address, authentication identifiers and session information needed to create and secure your account.

Receipt images and extracted information

When you choose to scan a receipt, the image is sent to our backend and an AI service to extract information such as the shop or supplier, date, total, VAT and category. You can review and edit the extracted information before saving it.

Expense records stored on your device

Saved folders, suppliers and expense records are currently stored locally on your device. They remain there until you delete them, clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Technical information

Our service providers may process basic technical information, security logs, device information and diagnostic data needed to operate, protect and improve the service.

3. Why we process information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To read receipt images and return extracted expense details.
  • To provide app features, security and support.
  • To diagnose errors, prevent abuse and maintain the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We use service providers that process information on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase, for authentication and backend functions.
  • Anthropic, for AI-assisted receipt image analysis.
  • Apple, for App Store and TestFlight distribution and related diagnostics.

These providers may process data in countries outside your own and apply their own security and retention practices under their service terms.

5. Receipt image retention

SnapAccounts does not intentionally save receipt images in its app database after the analysis request is completed. Images are transmitted for processing, and the service providers involved may retain limited data temporarily in accordance with their policies, legal obligations and security practices.

6. Data retention

Account information is kept while your account remains active and for any additional period required for security, legal or legitimate operational reasons. Locally saved expense records are controlled through your device.

7. Account and data deletion

You can delete individual expense records from the app where available. To request deletion of your SnapAccounts account and associated account data, follow the instructions on our account deletion page or email hello@menoova.it from the email address connected to the account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to receive a copy of certain data. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

SnapAccounts is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online or device-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

12. Contact

Email: hello@menoova.it
