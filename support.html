How can we help?

Support for SnapAccounts, account access and receipt scanning.

← Back to SnapAccounts

Contact support

Describe the problem, the device you are using and what happened immediately before the issue. Please do not email full card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information.

Email hello@menoova.it

Camera permission

On iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and make sure SnapAccounts is enabled.

Receipt not recognised

Use good lighting, keep the receipt flat and make sure the total and date are readable. Long receipts can be captured in more than one photo.

Incorrect information

Every scanned field can be changed before saving. Always check the date, total and VAT.

Account access

Use the password reset option from the login screen. For further help, contact us from the email connected to your account.

Account deletion

Instructions for requesting account and associated account-data deletion are available on the Delete Account page.

Response times

We aim to respond to support requests within five working days.
