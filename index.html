What information can SnapAccounts read?+

The app can extract the shop or supplier, date, total, VAT and a suggested expense category from a receipt image.

Can I correct the scanned information?+

Yes. Every extracted field is shown for review and can be edited before you save the expense.

Can I separate personal and business spending?+

Yes. SnapAccounts includes separate Personal and Business areas, each with its own folders, suppliers and totals.

Can I export my records?+

Yes. You can export expense records as a CSV file that opens in Excel and can be shared with your accountant.
